title: Why Computers Use Zero and One
translationKey: pochemu-komyuter-ispolzuet-imenno-0-i-1
---
Because a real electrical signal is never perfectly precise: voltage changes slightly due to interference, heating, and component characteristics.

For example, a circuit can agree to treat a voltage below 0.8 V as zero and above 2 V as one. If the signal changes slightly, it will still be recognized correctly. The gap between these levels helps protect against errors.

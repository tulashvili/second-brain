In [this post](https://t.me/GoToTShaped/110), a developer describes, in two parts, how their team took part in a hackathon.

What I especially liked was what they built. They were given a headband and a short piece of documentation for it:

![[Pasted image 20250202230546.png]]

They wrote a library in C++ and used Golang on the server. Using several metrics, they tried to detect when a person's concentration dropped and block trading on Binance at that point.

What inspired me was that they finished a **physical device** you could actually use after writing the code. That's cool!

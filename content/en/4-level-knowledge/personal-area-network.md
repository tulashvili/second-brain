title: Personal Area Network (PAN)
---

A personal area network (PAN) is, roughly speaking, the network around one person. It can include Wi-Fi, IoT devices, and Bluetooth devices.

### Examples of PANs

1. **Home theater:** A phone sends video to a TV while headphones connect to the phone for private listening.
2. **Workout:** A fitness band sends activity data to a phone over Bluetooth, while wireless headphones play music from the same phone.
3. **Smart home:** A laptop or tablet controls lights, thermostats, and sensors over Wi-Fi or Zigbee. A voice assistant can receive spoken commands through its microphone and act on connected devices.

A computer chip consists of interconnected electronic components, primarily [[транзисторы|transistors]]. It receives an [[electrical-signal|electrical signal]], processes it, and passes it on.

The circuit measures voltage at its input and determines which value it represents: **0 or 1**. You can think of such a circuit as a very fast switch with predefined rules.

title: Proxy
---

A proxy is a server positioned in front of a group of client computers. When those clients request websites or Internet services, the proxy intercepts the requests and contacts the destination servers on their behalf.

![[Pasted image 20230712095333.png]]

A proxy can be used to:

- **Work around browsing restrictions.** A forward proxy lets users connect to the proxy rather than directly to the sites they visit.
- **Block selected content.** A proxy can deny access to specified sites for a group of users.

Related topics include forward proxies, reverse proxies, and the different types of proxy servers.

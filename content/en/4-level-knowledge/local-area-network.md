> [!tldr] In brief
> A LAN connects devices in a small area, such as a home or an office.

A local area network (LAN) usually covers a small area, such as a home, office, or organization. A home Wi-Fi network is one example.

### Examples of LANs in everyday life

1. **Home:** Family members share files, photos, music, printers, and other devices.
2. **Office:** Employees work on shared projects and access common documents, databases, and server applications.
3. **Education:** Teachers and students access materials on a school server and use the network for online tests and lectures.
4. **Healthcare:** Doctors can access patient records stored on a hospital server.

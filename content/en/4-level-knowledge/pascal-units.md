A **unit** is a separate program component with ready-made procedures and functions. A unit is included with `uses`:
```pascal
uses GraphABC;
```

By purpose, units are similar to libraries in other programming languages, but terminology depends on the language.
In Pascal, a unit is a separate code component included through `uses`.
In other languages, a similar role can be performed by **libraries**, **packages**, **modules**, or **namespaces**. These concepts do not always correspond exactly in their structure.

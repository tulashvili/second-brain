This is a [[number-systems|number system]] in which we can use digits from 1 to 9.
It is a positional system, which means that the value represented by each digit depends on its position.
For example, take the number 352:

$1 \cdot 10^3$ + $7 \cdot 10^2$ + $0 \cdot 10^1$ + $2 \cdot 10^0$

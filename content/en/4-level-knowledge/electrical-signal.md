Put simply, it is [[электричество|electricity]] that has been “configured” to carry a message from one piece of hardware to another.

Imagine a water pipe:

- If water flows steadily with the same force, it is not a signal.
- If you quickly open and close the tap in Morse code or a rhythm, the water flow becomes a signal.

In electricity, current flows or voltage changes in wires instead of water. A receiver—a speaker, display, or processor—detects those changes and understands whether to light a pixel, play a sound, or open a door.

## Main types of electrical signals

[[how-computers-process-information#^bvidl7]]

- An analog signal changes smoothly and continuously, like waves on water. Old radio and microphones work this way: voice volume and pitch become smooth voltage oscillations.
- A digital signal is transmitted as distinct steps or pulses with only two states: “current present” (one, 1) and “no current” (zero, 0). Computers and smartphones work on this principle.

Roman numeral system
- I, V, and X = 1, 5, and 10
- Any number from 1 to 49 can be written

Sumerian numeral system
- It taught us to divide fractions into 60 parts
- Sexagesimal system


Arabic numeral system
- The system most familiar to us
- It is the [[decimal-number-system]]
